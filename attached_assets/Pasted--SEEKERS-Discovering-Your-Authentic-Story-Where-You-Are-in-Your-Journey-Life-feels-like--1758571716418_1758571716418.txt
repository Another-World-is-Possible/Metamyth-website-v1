## WEBSITE INTERFACE NAVIGATION INSTRUCTIONS

### Initial Landing Page
Create three prominent pathway buttons arranged as stations on a visual journey timeline:
- **SEEKERS** - "Discovering Your Authentic Story"
- **CHANGEMAKERS** - "Intensive Transformation Through Fellowship"  
- **WORLD BUILDERS** - "Scaling Transformation to Planetary Impact"

No pricing displayed on initial buttons.

### User Interaction Flow
1. **Identity Recognition**: When user clicks any station button, that station expands showing the journey description and how you serve at that level
2. **Value Demonstration**: Detailed tier information slides in, emphasizing transformation and community
3. **Investment Revelation**: Pricing appears 2/3 down the expanded panel, after emotional investment is established
4. **Commitment Escalation**: "What is your story worth to you?" header reframes price as self-valuation
5. **Altruistic Upselling**: Interactive "+$600" buttons with "Bring [X] others with you" counter that updates
6. **Payment Accessibility**: Full investment shown first, then payment plan options immediately below
7. **Final Commitment**: "Claim Your Author's Seat" button appears after investment selection

### Visual Design Elements
- Journey path connects all three stations horizontally
- Each station has unique icon (compass, bridge, mountain peak)
- Pricing remains hidden until deep engagement with tier content
- Clean, scannable layout prioritizing identity match over cost comparison
- "First cohort - 50 spots - Last opportunity this year" appears near bottom as transformational urgency

The interface should feel like discovering your calling rather than shopping for services. By the time they see investment amounts, they should be calculating the cost of staying stuck versus the value of transformation.
