## WEBSITE INTERFACE NAVIGATION INSTRUCTIONS

### Initial Landing Page
Create three prominent pathway buttons arranged as stations on a visual journey timeline:
- **SEEKERS** (left station) - "Discovering Your Authentic Story" - $1,200 baseline
- **CHANGEMAKERS** (center station) - "Intensive Transformation Through Fellowship" - $2,100 baseline  
- **WORLD BUILDERS** (right station) - "Scaling Transformation to Planetary Impact" - $4,200 baseline

### User Interaction Flow
1. **Station Selection**: When user clicks any station button, that station expands and becomes highlighted while others remain visible but dimmed
2. **Information Panel**: Detailed tier information slides in from the right side of screen showing the complete description for selected tier
3. **Cross-Navigation**: User can click other station buttons to compare options without losing their place
4. **Commitment Selector**: Within each tier panel, include interactive element asking "What is your story worth to you?" with baseline amount displayed and "+" buttons to add $600 increments
5. **Scholarship Counter**: Show "Bring [X] others with you" that updates as they increase their investment
6. **Payment Options**: Display both full payment and plan options (2x or 3x payments) that update based on their selected amount
7. **Proceed Button**: "Claim Your Author's Seat" button appears once they've selected their tier and investment amount

### Visual Design Elements
- Journey path connects all three stations horizontally across page
- Each station has unique icon representing its stage (compass for Seekers, bridge for Changemakers, mountain peak for World Builders)
- Subtle animations show progression from left to right
- Clean, scannable layout with expandable sections for details
- Mobile-responsive design where timeline becomes vertical stack

The interface should feel like selecting your starting position on an epic quest rather than choosing a product package.
